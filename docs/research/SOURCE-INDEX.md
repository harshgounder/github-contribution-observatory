## source handling rules

- primary source first;
- source date and revision recorded;
- search snippets are discovery aids, not final evidence;
- unconfirmed audit reports are labeled as such;
- public benchmark claims are not treated as private or current ground truth;
- every claim must state whether it is measured, reported, estimated, self-attested, independently regraded, or unresolved.
