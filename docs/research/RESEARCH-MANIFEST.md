## method

three passes:

1. source and method discovery;
2. independent technical challenge and benchmark audit;
3. synthesis into contracts and acceptance gates.

paired experiments used or proposed:

- no retrieval versus retrieved context;
- actual evidence versus oracle evidence;
- original evidence versus perturbed evidence;
- raw patch versus normalized patch;
- reported cost versus independently metered cost;
- historical benchmark versus fresh task set.
